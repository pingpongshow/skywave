# Skywave v1.2.5 — Release Notes

A fix release: ULTRA sessions connect as ULTRA, promotions cost less
airtime, and a burst decoded from two captures is processed once.
Skywave has never been released, so no compatibility constraint applies,
but **both ends of a link should run v1.2.5**: the promotion exchange
changed transport.

## What changed

- **ULTRA handshake.** `modem.ultra_enable` never reached the handshake
  policy, so an ULTRA responder silently ignored every ULTRA CALL and
  sessions connected only after the caller's family fallback to WIDE
  (~250 s). Every "ULTRA" soak result before this release, v1.2.4's
  included, was a WIDE session. True ULTRA, floor 9 dB (seeds 1-6): 6/6,
  97 KB; churn (seeds 11-13): 3/3, 119 KB A->B.
- **Promotions ride the target mode.** A promotion's MODE_CHANGE and
  MODE_ACK now go out at the mode being promoted to instead of the
  current one; a promotion the channel cannot carry is refused by its own
  missing MODE_ACK, and demotions stay on the robust tier. On NARROW every
  robust-tier frame is >= 20.7 s, so an M7 -> M8 step was two robust
  frames and the Moderate floor spent more airtime on mode control than on
  DATA. Floor, six seeds: NARROW 9 dB 26.0 -> 30.4 KB and 5/6 -> 6/6, WIDE
  97 -> 102 KB, others unchanged within noise; churn 3/3 every family.
- **One burst, two captures.** The detector could open a second capture on
  a burst it already had (a fast-tier ACK also matched the 12-rep CSS
  pattern at quality 0.04), and both decodes were dispatched: SNR and CFO
  reports applied twice, the classifier fed twice. The receiver now
  reports its sync point and the engine drops a repeat at the same
  position; an identical ACK sent back to back is still processed.

## Measured and not adopted

- A lighter robust header for M5-M7: costs 1-3 dB on every family's floors
  because the header doubles as the payload's channel training
  (`docs/conformance/header_spread_2026-10-15/`).
- Faster loss recovery (requeue on a POLL-answering ACK) and a tail-loss
  probe: at or below baseline almost everywhere over 6 floor + 5 churn
  seeds per family. Details in ROADMAP.

## Testing note

The two-modem soak world is chaotic down to single on-air bits: one
changed reserved flag bit moved a six-seed total by ~20% with identical
logic. Effects smaller than that need well over six seeds
(`docs/TESTING.md`).

## Known limitations

- NARROW on the Moderate floor at 6 dB passes 4/6 seeds: the link lives
  at M5-M7, whose 20 s robust frames are the price of the header's
  training role.
- Simulation results; on-air confirmation is pending.
- No LICENSE file (owner's hold).
