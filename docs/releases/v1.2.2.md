# Skywave v1.2.2 — Release Notes

Receiver and link-adaptation fixes measured after v1.2.1. **The wire
format is unchanged (`kWireVersion` and `kProtocolVersion` both 1);
v1.2.2 interoperates with v1.2.1, v1.2.0, v1.1.0 and v1.0.0.** No
configuration changes are needed.

## What changed, and why

### AMC: CCIR Good no longer read as Severe

Since v1.2.0 the AMC delivered 0.80 of the oracle on a CCIR Good SNR
ramp. The cause was the channel classifier, not the AMC margins. Its
Severe gate averaged delay spread with an EMA. On CCIR Good, 26% of
decoded frames mis-estimate spread at ~5 ms (median 0.62 ms), so the EMA
crossed 1.6 ms. With Good's genuine SNR variance the verdict went
Severe, and the AMC steered by the CCIR Poor table, demoting
M11 → M9 → M8 → M7 at 18 dB with zero retries.

- The Severe gate reads the median spread of the last 9 frames (the
  EMA is kept for the Static gate).
- Promotion into M11–M13 needs +0.5 dB margin (other rungs −1.5 dB),
  so the now-unblocked climb does not land in a failing top rung.

Simulation: M3 ramp 0.80 → 0.93 of the oracle, static ramp
0.76 → 0.77, churn +5..8% over six seeds, coupled churn +17%, Moderate
and Poor floor soaks byte-identical, every soak PASS.

### CSS deep tier under LO drift (ROADMAP 8.8)

The receiver removes the preamble's CFO once. Under drift the residual
becomes, through the chirp's delay-Doppler coupling, a timing offset
that grows with 2^SF, so the SF8 pilots mis-time the SF5–SF7 payload.
MID/NARROW M1–M3 failed 88–100% at 0.1 Hz/s. When a payload fails, the
receiver now searches the drift rate (±0.2 Hz/s), derotates the
payload's quadratic phase, re-runs the pilot/demod pass, and lets the
CRC pick. Frames that decode at the first pass are untouched.

AWGN floor+3 at 0.1 Hz/s: MID M2/M3 100% → 0%, NARROW M0–M3
88–100% → 0–4%; WIDE unchanged; no drift: AWGN identical, CCIR Moderate
equal or better at every point.

### OFDM robust frames under extreme in-frame drift

The in-frame FLL is first-order, so a linear drift r leaves a steady
frequency lag of r·Tsym/gain. At the robust gain (0.1) the 20–40 s
robust frames lost their headers. A higher gain everywhere feeds CCIR
Poor's Doppler back (8.6), so a frame that fails at the class gain is
walked once more at gain 0.3, without re-feeding HARQ.

AWGN floor+6, 40 trials, without → with: NARROW M4 @ 1 Hz/s 25% → 2.5%,
MID M4 @ 2 Hz/s 82.5% → 2.5%; 1 Hz/s ≤ 2.5% on every family M4–M7.
CCIR Poor/Moderate: no point worse, several better.

### A/B switches (measurement only)

`SKW_CHANCLASS_SPREAD_EMA=1`, `SKW_AMC_PROMOTE_MARGIN_HIGH`,
`SKW_CSS_DRIFT_SEARCH=0`, `SKW_FLL_DRIFT_RETRY=0` / `SKW_FLL_GAIN_DRIFT`
(CONFIGURATION.md).

## Costs

Both drift fixes spend CPU only on frames that already failed: about
1 s per failing NARROW CSS M2 frame (64 s on air), and one extra OFDM
walk per failing OFDM frame (+8–11% on a floor-level sweep). It is
worker-thread time.

## Known limitations

See ROADMAP "Open-item review (2026-10-12)".

- **Drift beyond the gates:** CSS NARROW at 0.2 Hz/s fails 32–68%;
  OFDM NARROW M4/M5 at 2 Hz/s (80 Hz inside a 40 s frame) 10–17.5%.
- **CSS MID M0 on CCIR Moderate** fails floor+1 (27.5%), unchanged
  (CSS is gated on AWGN).
- **Reverse-direction offset after a retune:** harmless inside ±55 Hz.
- **A mid-frame timing slip costs the frame** (optional item).
- **The AMC and drift fixes are simulation results;** on-air
  confirmation is pending.
- **No LICENSE file** (owner's hold).
